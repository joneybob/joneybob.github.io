Bellwether

Election atlas & forecast · Solo · 2026

An atlas of every certified election return in American history, down to the county and precinct. Anyone can pull up past results, compare places and simulate their own outcome. I built the data pipeline, the model and the interface.

Every result is collected from state and county sources, cleaned and joined to Census and polling data, then shipped as one fast web app with a full methodology paper behind it.

Focs

Focus app for iPhone & Mac · Solo · 2025–now

I wanted one app that blocks distractions, knows my class schedule and shows whether I'm actually getting work done. Nothing did all three, so I built it.

Focs runs Pomodoro and Flowtime sessions, blocks apps with Screen Time, plans the day around your classes and syncs to a Mac app with end-to-end encryption. Friends can see each other's focus time.

SwiftSwiftUICloudKitScreen Time APIWidgetKit

Pomodoro runs 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break.

Flowtime counts up until you stop. Your break is a fifth of the time you focused, so a long session earns a longer rest.

frmat

Feed planner for iPhone & iPad · Solo · 2025–2026

frmat lets you design your social feed before you post. You can drag posts around a grid, split one photo into a seamless 3, 6 or 9-post layout and preview exactly how your profile will look.

Everything runs on the device, with no account and nothing uploaded. The App Store listing, privacy label and review notes are written and ready.

St. Elmo Late Night

Web app for Delta Phi · 2026

Running a late night at the house used to take a group chat, a spreadsheet and a lot of yelling. I built one app that handles all of it.

Brothers sign up for roles, add their guests and see who is up next. The door gets a searchable guest list, and the social team gets checklists and a record of no-shows. It installs on a phone like a native app.

About

The summer after my junior year of high school, I worked at Berkeley Lab on battery storage and grid emissions. I learned R, worked with very large data sets and got one piece of advice I still use: find the intersection between your talents and your passions.

For me, that intersection is data and politics. I study Mathematical Economics at Penn, with minors in Statistics & Data Science and Political Science, and I spend most of my free time building tools that make elections easier to understand.

I'm from Berkeley, California, where I rowed crew on the Oakland Estuary and co-founded a cleanup crew to take care of it.
