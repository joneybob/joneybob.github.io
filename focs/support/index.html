Focs Support

Help with the Focs app for iPhone and iPad

Contact. Email j.create100@gmail.com with what happened and your iOS version. You can also use Settings > Contact Support in the app. Replies usually come within two days.

App blocking doesn't work

  • Focs blocks apps with Apple's Screen Time. The first time you turn blocking on, iOS asks for permission; tap Continue and confirm with Face ID or your passcode.
  • If you said no, turn it on again in Settings > Focus Shield in Focs, or in the iPhone Settings app under Screen Time.
  • Choose the apps to block before you start a session. Blocking lasts until the session ends.
  • Strict Mode stops you from ending a session early. You can turn it off when no session is running.

Purchases

  • Focus sessions are unlimited and free. Focs Pro adds Strict Mode, automatic class lockdown, week planning, full stats history, Focus Spot reminders and calendar links, as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase.
  • If you bought it before or on another device, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.
  • To cancel the yearly subscription, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Friends

  • Friends is off until you turn it on, and it needs you to be signed in to iCloud.
  • Add someone with their 6-character code. They see your name, weekly focus time, streak and best day.
  • To report or block someone, open their page in Friends. Blocking removes them and stops them coming back. If you don't want them to see you either, reset your code from the same page.

Calendars and classes

  • Add classes by hand in Classes, or import repeating events from your calendar.
  • To add a Google Calendar or Canvas calendar, copy its iCal link and paste it in Settings > Calendar > Add calendar link.
  • In Calendar, hold and drag on the day to mark free time, and Focs plans your tasks into it.

Deleting your data

In Focs, go to Settings > Data > Delete my data. If you signed in with Apple, you can also use Delete Account on the Account page. Both remove everything Focs stored on your device and in iCloud, including your Friends profile.

Privacy

Focs has no ads, analytics or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
