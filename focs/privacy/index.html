Focs Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Short version. Focs has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your timer history, tasks and schedule stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Friends can see your focus totals only if you turn Friends on.

What stays on your device

  • Focus sessions, stats, streaks, tasks, classes, schedules and settings.
  • The apps and sites you choose to block. Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time on your device; Focs cannot see which apps you use.
  • Your focus spots and your location. Location is checked on your device to notice when you arrive at a spot you saved.
  • Your name, if you use Sign in with Apple. Focs does not receive or store your email address.

What is stored in iCloud

  • Sync. If you sync between your iPhone and Mac, your data is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Only devices you have paired can read it. The developer cannot.
  • Friends (optional, off by default). When you turn Friends on, Focs publishes a profile to CloudKit with your display name, your friend code, your focus minutes (today, this week, the last seven days, total, best day) and your streak. Anyone you give your code to can see it.
  • Spot sharing (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, friends you approve can see a pin for a spot you marked shareable while you are in a session there. The location is encrypted so that only those friends can read it. Focs never shares your live location.
  • Invites. When you add a friend's code, Focs records that your account was invited by theirs, so they can earn the invite reward. The record holds two anonymous iCloud account identifiers and nothing else.

Calendars

If you connect a calendar or paste a calendar link (for example from Google Calendar or Canvas), Focs downloads your events straight to your device to plan around them. They are not sent anywhere else.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Focs learns only whether you have Focs Pro. It never sees your payment details.

What Focs does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No servers run by the developer. Everything that leaves your device goes to Apple's iCloud.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can remove iCloud data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage. To have your Friends profile and invite record deleted, email the address below with your friend code and it will be removed.

Children

Focs is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
